Primary roles stay fixed. On Demo Day, anyone answers anything, so backup roles are how we learn each other's parts.

## How we decide

Small implementation details within an already agreed plan are handled by the member doing the work. Project decisions, including design, features, specifications, scope, stack, and pivots, require consensus: all three members must agree.

## When we work

Weekly sync: no fixed day or time; meetings are arranged flexibly in the Microsoft Teams chat. Core hours: no fixed range; each member monitors the Teams chat and responds within 6 hours, except in exceptional circumstances.

## Contribution

Work is visible in commits and the journal. If someone carries or someone coasts, we say it in week terms, not in Week 15. Each member is responsible for completing the work assigned to their fixed primary role.

## When it breaks

Step 1: name the issue inside the team within 48 hours. Step 2: if it remains unresolved for one week, bring it to the instructor together. No-show rule: after three warnings for missing agreed meetings without notice, the team informs the instructor.
